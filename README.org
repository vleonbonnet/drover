#+TITLE: drover
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil

Drover gathers the sessions of your coding agents into one place in Emacs: which ones wait for you, which ones run, and which finished while you were elsewhere. It shows how many sessions need you in the mode line, takes you to the most urgent one, and lists every session in one list, in a window or beside your work, from which you can open, answer, interrupt, archive or preview any of them.

Agent front ends plug in as backends: [[https://github.com/vleonbonnet/opencode.el][opencode.el]] for [[https://opencode.ai/][OpenCode]] 2, [[https://github.com/xenodium/agent-shell][agent-shell]] for its ACP agents, and [[https://github.com/manzaltu/claude-code-ide.el][claude-code-ide]] for Claude Code in a terminal. One prompt buffer, =*drover*=, writes to any of them: it gathers context from where you work, lets you choose the agent, model, effort and directory, and sends to a running session or starts a new one.

* Requirements

- Emacs 30.1, a Nerd Font for the status icons, [[https://github.com/magit/transient][transient]] for the menus, and [[https://github.com/magit/magit][magit-section]] for the session list.
- For OpenCode sessions, opencode.el with its =opencode-state= session registry.
- For ACP agents (Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, Gemini CLI), agent-shell; for Claude Code in a terminal, claude-code-ide, with [[https://github.com/dakra/ghostel][ghostel]] for its statuses. Each backend loads with its front end.

* Installation

With elpaca or straight:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (use-package drover
    :ensure (:host github :repo "vleonbonnet/drover")
    :hook (elpaca-after-init . drover-attention-mode)
    :bind ("C-'" . drover-dispatch))
#+end_src

The OpenCode backend loads with opencode.el. Its sessions come from opencode.el's registry, which follows the shared OpenCode service whether or not a session has a buffer; drover opens the service's event stream when it starts. opencode.el's hints then name drover's keys, such as "C-' A answers it" for a pending question, through =opencode-command-keys-function=, unless you set that option yourself.

* Statuses

| Icon (Nerd Font)     | Status    | Meaning                                                 |
|----------------------+-----------+---------------------------------------------------------|
| =nf-md-help_circle=  | Needs you | A permission request or question waits for you          |
| =nf-fa-times_circle= | Failed    | A turn failed while you were not looking at the session |
| =nf-md-check_circle= | Done      | A turn ended while you were not looking at the session  |
| =nf-fa-refresh=      | Retrying  | The running turn waits to retry a failed request        |
| =nf-md-timer_sand=   | Working   | A turn runs                                             |
| =nf-fa-circle_o=     | Idle      | Nothing to do                                           |

=drover-status-glyphs= changes them; the icons need a Nerd Font. In the list, the icons of Working and Retrying are animated (see [[*Animated icons][Animated icons]]).

The first three need you (=drover-attention-statuses=). Sessions are ranked by =drover-status-order=, and among those that need you the longest wait comes first. Whether you saw a finished turn is the backend's to know: OpenCode records it in the service, so a turn read in any client, the TUI, the desktop app or Emacs, is no longer Done.

Subagent sessions show under their parent, indented. Which of them show, =drover-show-subagents= says, and =U= in the list cycles it:

| Mode     | Shows                                                                                          |
|----------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =all=    | every subagent                                                                                 |
| =active= | the subagents in the middle of a turn: working, waiting to retry, or waiting for you (default) |
| =none=   | no subagent                                                                                    |

The list's header tells how many subagents are hidden, the mode, and what =U= does next, such as =Subagents: active  U hides all=; its mode line adds =+subagents= or =-subagents= while not on =active=. A subagent whose turn ended, unseen or not, is not active: it hides by default, and its parent no longer stays among the Done sessions because of it. A parent goes where the most urgent session of its visible tree belongs, so one whose subagent waits for you is listed with the sessions that need you, the subagent beneath it; a hidden subagent never places its parent in a group. A subagent matching the filter shows under its parent whatever the mode, even =none=, since it explains why its tree is listed. The mode-line count and =drover-next= count every session, subagents included, whatever the mode.

Shown or not, a session whose subagents (at any depth) are active says so beside its status icon:

| Icon (Nerd Font)        | Means                            |
|-------------------------+----------------------------------|
| =nf-md-robot_confused=  | subagents wait for you           |
| =nf-md-robot=, animated | subagents work, or wait to retry |

Each is followed by how many when more than one, such as =? *2= for one waiting and two working; their tooltip names them. =drover-subagent-glyphs= changes the icons. This is how your OpenCode session shows that a subagent of its runs while it is itself idle.

* Attention

=drover-attention-mode= shows one count per status that needs you in the mode line, one icon and count per status, and nothing when no session needs you. Its tooltip lists the sessions; clicking it runs =drover-next=, the right button =drover-select=.

- =drover-next= visits the most urgent session. Pressed again at once, it moves to the next one in the order of the first press, so a session changing status meanwhile does not reshuffle the round.
- =drover-select= picks any session with completion, grouped by status and annotated with its project, branch, age and what it waits for. With a prefix argument it opens the session in another window.
- =drover-mark-all-seen= marks every Done and Failed session seen after confirming how many (=drover-catch-up-statuses=); with a numeric prefix argument, only those that ended at least that many hours ago. Useful to start from a clean slate, or after a day away.

* Keys

Drover binds no global key. Its commands are gathered in one menu, =drover-dispatch=; bind it to a key you can reach from anywhere, for instance:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (keymap-global-set "C-'" #'drover-dispatch)
#+end_src

or, with =use-package=, =:bind ("C-'" . drover-dispatch)=. The keys come in three sets, and no key belongs to two, so the menu holds them all: the global keys everywhere, the session keys in lists of sessions and in prompt buffers, the prompt keys in prompt buffers. What does not apply where you are is greyed out rather than hidden, so the menu keeps its shape. With the OpenCode backend, it reads:

#+begin_example
  Global
   j Next          l List              a Agent       C Compact
   b Select        L Side list         m Model       f Fork
   " Prompt        p Project           e Effort      . Last answer
   ' Show prompt   S Search sessions   d Directory   O Open link

  Session
   o Other window   A Answer        r Rename    U Subagents
   u Parent         K Interrupt     x Delete    V View
   c Subagent       s Mark seen     z Archive
   v Peek           + New session
                    g Refresh

  Prompt
   RET Send              / Slash command   t Revert here
   S-<return> Schedule   @ Mention         T Cancel revert
   i Attach              y Copy block
   k Clear
#+end_example

** Session keys

In every list of sessions (drover's list, and the lists of other front ends that turn on =drover-session-keys-mode=) these keys act on the session at point directly; in a prompt buffer, such as an OpenCode session buffer, they act on its session through the menu.

| Key | Command                    | Action                                            |
|-----+----------------------------+---------------------------------------------------|
| =o= | =drover-open-other-window= | open it in another window                         |
| =A= | =drover-respond=           | answer its question or permission request         |
| =K= | =drover-interrupt=         | interrupt its turn, after confirmation            |
| =s= | =drover-mark-seen=         | mark its ended turn seen                          |
| =u= | =drover-open-parent=       | open its parent session                           |
| =c= | =drover-open-subagent=     | pick one of its subagent sessions and open it     |
| =r= | =drover-rename=            | rename it                                         |
| =x= | =drover-delete=            | delete it for good, after confirmation            |
| =z= | =drover-archive=           | archive it, after confirmation                    |
| =g= | =drover-refresh=           | refresh the list, or reload the session's buffer  |
| =+= | =drover-new-session=       | start a session in its directory                  |
| =v= | =drover-peek=              | preview where it stands, without opening (lists)  |
| =U= | =drover-toggle-subagents=  | show all, active or no subagents, in turn (lists) |
| =V= | =drover-view=              | the View menu (drover's list)                     |

=RET= opens the session at point, and the usual keys of each list stay: =n= and =p= move, =TAB= folds, =q= quits. In lists drover does not draw, =drover-session-keys-mode= adds these keys. opencode.el lists no sessions of its own: drover's list shows them.

To store an Org link to the session at point, use Org's own =org-store-link= (=C-c l=) with a link type of your own whose store function reads =drover-session-at-point=.

** The menu beside the list

The session keys of the menu act on the session the current buffer tells: the one at point in a list, the buffer's own in a session buffer. In a buffer telling no session, such as the prompt, =*drover*=, or a file you edit, while drover's list shows in a window of the frame (the most recently used, if several), they act on that list instead: on the session at point of the list's window, as if typed there, and so do the list's own entries, =v=, =U= and =V=. A session buffer keeps acting on its own session. The global and prompt keys keep to the current buffer.

The List group, whether the menu opened in the list or beside it, moves the point of the list's window, the menu staying open and following the new session at point: its title names it, and what applies to it is greyed out anew. Follow mode shows each session as you go, and the list highlights it, as moving in the list would.

*** The List group

While the menu acts on drover's list, here or beside, it shows a fourth group, named after the session at point; there =RET= opens that session rather than send, so the Prompt group leaves it out:

#+begin_example
  Prompt
   S-<return> Schedule   / Slash command   t Revert here
   i Attach              @ Mention         T Cancel revert
   k Clear               y Copy block

  List: Fix the login redirect
   C-n Next session       M-n Next group       M-< First session   RET Open
   C-p Previous session   M-p Previous group   M-> Last session
#+end_example

| Key   | Command                        | Action                                          |
|-------+--------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------|
| =C-n= | =drover-list-next-session=     | the next session, over group headings           |
| =C-p= | =drover-list-previous-session= | the previous session                            |
| =M-n= | =drover-list-next-group=       | the first session of the next group             |
| =M-p= | =drover-list-previous-group=   | the first session of the previous group         |
| =M-<= | =drover-list-first-session=    | the first session                               |
| =M->= | =drover-list-last-session=     | the last session in sight                       |
| =RET= | =drover-open-at-point=         | open the session at point, as =RET= in the list |

Sessions of collapsed groups are passed over. =RET= opens the session as =RET= in the list would: from a side window, in the window where you work; from an ordinary window, in that window, or with =C-u=, the menu's prefix argument, in another one. In a buffer sending prompts, such as =*drover*=, =RET= stays Send: a prompt lost to a slip of the finger costs more than reaching for =o=, which opens the list's session in another window. These keys bind nothing in the list itself, where Magit's =n=, =p=, =M-n= and =M-p= move.

** Prompt keys

In a prompt buffer, where you write to an agent, through the menu.

| Key     | Command                       | Action                                 |
|---------+-------------------------------+----------------------------------------|
| =RET=   | =drover-prompt-send=          | send the prompt                        |
| =S-RET= | =drover-prompt-schedule=      | schedule the prompt                    |
| =i=     | =drover-prompt-attach=        | attach the region, or a file or buffer |
| =k=     | =drover-prompt-clear=         | erase the prompt being written         |
| =/=     | =drover-prompt-slash=         | insert a slash command                 |
| =@=     | =drover-prompt-mention=       | mention a subagent                     |
| =y=     | =drover-prompt-copy-block=    | copy the code block at point           |
| =t=     | =drover-prompt-revert=        | stage a revert of what follows point   |
| =T=     | =drover-prompt-revert-cancel= | cancel the staged revert               |

** Global keys

Everywhere, through the menu.

| Key | Command                     | Action                                                         |
|-----+-----------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------|
| =j= | =drover-next=               | visit the most urgent session                                  |
| =b= | =drover-select=             | choose a session                                               |
| ="= | =drover-prompt-dwim=        | write a prompt, with the context at point                      |
| ='= | =drover-prompt-toggle=      | show or hide the prompt                                        |
| =l= | =drover-list=               | the list of sessions, in a window                              |
| =L= | =drover-list-side=          | the list of sessions beside your work, shown or hidden         |
| =p= | =drover-select-project=     | the list of the sessions of a project                          |
| =S= | =opencode-consult-sessions= | search the OpenCode session buffers                            |
| =a= | =drover-prompt-agent=       | choose the agent                                               |
| =m= | =drover-prompt-model=       | choose the model                                               |
| =e= | =drover-prompt-effort=      | choose the reasoning effort                                    |
| =d= | =drover-prompt-directory=   | move the session to another directory                          |
| =C= | =drover-compact=            | compact the conversation, or the session at point in a list    |
| =f= | =drover-fork=               | fork the conversation at point, or the whole session in a list |
| =.= | =drover-prompt-last-answer= | show the start of the last answer                              |
| =O= | =drover-prompt-open-link=   | open the link at point                                         |

=S= comes with the OpenCode backend. ="= is greyed out in the prompt itself. =p= offers the projects of the sessions drover knows, and lists those of the one you choose, through the filter. =a=, =m=, =e= and =d= act on the current prompt buffer, in a list on the open buffer of the session at point, else on the one =drover-default-prompt-buffer-function= returns, drover's prompt by default; =.= likewise, else on the prompt buffer you visited last. The prompt keys, and =O=, apply only in the prompt buffer itself, since they work on what you see there.

** Adding to the menu

Add your own commands with =transient-append-suffix= and =transient-insert-suffix=, placed relative to a key of the menu. These places stay put:

- after ='=, the last of drover's ways to pick a session or prompt, more of them, chained one after the other: =#= after ='=, and so on (after =b= too, before the prompt's);
- after =p=, more lists of sessions: the OpenCode backend appends =S= there;
- after =O=, more prompt commands.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (transient-append-suffix 'drover-dispatch "'"
    '("#" "Scheduled prompts" my-scheduled-prompts))
#+end_src

=drover-dispatch-session=, =drover-dispatch-list-p=, =drover-dispatch-view-p= and =drover-dispatch-prompt-p= are predicates for the =:inapt-if-not= of such entries.

* The list

=drover-list= shows every session in a Magit-style buffer, =*drover sessions*=, in an ordinary window; =drover-list-side= shows the same buffer in a side window beside your work (=drover-list-side-window-side=, =drover-list-side-window-width=), or hides it, and with a prefix argument moves between it and your work. Groups and sessions are sections: =TAB= folds a group, or shows the details of a session: its directory, agent, model, creation, prompt cache and every request it waits on. The Idle group starts collapsed (=drover-list-collapsed-groups=); what you collapse or expand survives refreshes.

From the side window, =RET= and =o= open the session at point in the window where you work, =C-o= shows it there and leaves you in the list, and follow mode shows each session there as point reaches it. Wherever the list is, the session shown in the window beside it is highlighted.

** What a line shows

The list is drawn in a proportional font (the face =drover-list=), laid out in pixels, and what a line shows depends on the width of its window. =drover-list-fields= ranks the fields a line may show and the room each needs:

| Field    | Shows                                                               | Rank | In two lines          |
|----------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+------+-----------------------|
| title    | the status icon, the icon of active subagents and the title, always |    0 | first line            |
| age      | how long it has been active, or needed you                          |    1 | first line, right     |
| cache    | the prompt cache's lifetime left                                    |    2 | the line's background |
| summary  | what it waits for                                                   |    3 | second line           |
| location | project and branch                                                  |    4 |                       |
| agent    | the agent                                                           |    5 |                       |
| model    | the model                                                           |    6 |                       |
| created  | how long ago it was created                                         |    7 |                       |

The list shows the fields with something to show, by rank, as long as they fit, as aligned columns, but those you hide (=drover-list-hidden-fields=, or the View menu's Columns). When not even the summary fits beside the title, age and cache, each session takes two lines instead: its icons, title and age, and under them, for a session that needs you, what it waits for. The layout is computed again whenever the window's width changes. One space parts each icon from the next and from the title, and columns stay aligned to the pixel whatever the icons before them.

** Prompt caches

A session whose provider still holds its prompt cache shows how much of the cache's lifetime is left: in columns, as a short bar and the time left, such as =4m=; in two lines, as the background of its line (the face =drover-cache-bar=), filled from the left and shrinking as the cache runs out. An expired or unknown cache shows nothing. While the list is shown and a cache is live, a timer redraws the caches alone, every =drover-list-cache-interval= seconds.

** Animated icons

The icons of Working and Retrying sessions, and the icon of working subagents, are animated in the list: each shows the frames of =drover-animations= in turn, by default an hourglass whose sand runs, a clock whose hand turns, and a robot that blinks (Nerd Font icons, any string will do). Every frame takes the room of the widest, so nothing after it moves in the proportional font. As for prompt caches, a timer changes only how those few characters display, every =drover-list-animation-interval= seconds, half a second by default, and only while a shown list animates something; =nil= keeps the icons still.

** The View menu

=V= opens the View menu, which stays open as you change its settings and redraws the list at once:

#+begin_example
  Sort by                    Group by   Filter by       Toggles
   o urgency                  g status   p project any   F follow [off|on]
   r [ascending|descending]              s status any    U subagents [all|active|none]
                                         a agent any     O older [hidden|shown]
                                         m model any
                                         d updated any
                                         f text any
                                         c clear

  Columns
   -l location    -a agent    -m model    -c created    -t age    -p cache    -s summary
#+end_example

- Sort by urgency, the most urgent first, by title, project, branch, agent, model, last activity or creation, either way. Subagents stay under their parent: the trees are sorted, then the subagents of each session among themselves.
- Group by status, project, branch, agent, model, last activity (today, yesterday, this week, older), or nothing.
- Filter by project, status, agent, model, last activity (today, the last 24 hours, 7 or 30 days, or any age such as =3d=) and a text found in the title, summary, project or branch. An empty answer removes a filter; =c= clears them all. A subagent that matches shows under its parent, even while subagents are hidden.
- Toggle follow mode, which subagents show (all, the active ones, none: =U= cycles them, as in the list), and older sessions: those a backend keeps beyond the ones it follows, such as every unarchived OpenCode session. They are fetched when shown, and again by =g=.
- Choose the columns: each switch lets a field show, highlighted, or hides it, dimmed, at once; the list then chooses by width among the fields shown. The title always shows. The switches change =drover-list-hidden-fields= for the session; customize it to keep your choice.

The list redraws when sessions change while it is displayed; hidden, it waits until it is shown again, and costs nothing meanwhile.

* The prompt

=C-' "= (=drover-prompt-dwim=) opens =*drover*=, an Org buffer, with a link to the line at point and the region quoted in a code block; called again from elsewhere, it adds that context where you left point in the prompt. On an Org heading it starts a planning prompt for that task instead (=drover-prompt-task-format=, =drover-prompt-task-agent=), asking before it replaces a draft. =C-' '= (=drover-prompt-toggle=) shows or hides the prompt.

The header line shows what the prompt would run: directory, agent, model and effort, dim when inherited from the engine's defaults, bold when chosen with =C-' d=, =a=, =m= and =e=. From another buffer these keys choose for the next prompt. =a= switches directly when only two agents are offered.

=C-' RET= sends: it asks for the target among the running sessions, then the new sessions of =drover-prompt-new-targets=, each labelled with what it would run; in that picker =C-'= changes the options. Explicit choices are applied to the target before the prompt goes out. With an active region, only the region is sent and the rest stays for another send. A send that sends nothing, cancelled by a check, leaves the prompt as it was. =C-' k= clears the text and keeps the choices; =C-' i= attaches a buffer, quoted, or a project file, linked.

* Engines

An engine is the agent program a session runs: =opencode=, =claude-code=, =codex= or =gemini-cli=. Engines, not front ends, know what a new session runs: their models, their startup defaults (read from Claude Code's settings layers, Codex's =config.toml=, OpenCode's catalogs), their agents, and when they last took a prompt, which picks the engine the header shows. Each front end registers the engines it runs, so agent-shell can run any of them while claude-code-ide runs Claude Code only. =drover-claude-code-oauth-catalog= also lists the models of your Claude account.

Two front ends may show one conversation, such as an OpenCode session open in opencode.el and in agent-shell: drover lists it once, from the front end whose buffer you used last.

* Backends

A backend registers with =drover-register-backend=, giving functions that list its sessions as =drover-session= records and act on them (open, answer, interrupt, archive, mark seen, preview, start, rename, delete, fork, compact), fetch its older sessions (=:history=), and reports changes with =drover-backend-changed=. A record names its engine and the engine's identifier of the conversation (=engine=, =engine-id=), and may tell when the session's prompt cache expires and how long it lasts (=cache-expires=, =cache-ttl=). For the prompt it may offer =:engines=, =:create=, =:check=, =:apply-options=, =:send=, =:options= and =:settable=; an engine registers with =drover-register-engine= and its models with =drover-register-model-source=. See =doc/design.org=.

A front end also fits its buffers into the keys. A list of sessions sets =drover-session-at-point-function= and turns on =drover-session-keys-mode=; a prompt buffer sets =drover-session-at-point-function= to its own session and declares the prompt actions it implements with =drover-prompt-define=, such as =(drover-prompt-define 'send #'my-send)=.
