#+TITLE: drover
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil

Drover gathers the sessions of your coding agents into one place in Emacs: which ones wait for you, which ones run, and which finished while you were elsewhere. It shows how many sessions need you in the mode line, takes you to the most urgent one, and lists every session, in a window, in a sidebar beside your work, or both at once, from which you can open, answer, interrupt, archive, restore, fork, compact, delete or preview any of them, as far as its front end can.

Agent front ends plug in as backends: [[https://github.com/vleonbonnet/opencode.el][opencode.el]] for [[https://opencode.ai/][OpenCode]] 2, [[https://github.com/xenodium/agent-shell][agent-shell]] for its ACP agents, and [[https://github.com/manzaltu/claude-code-ide.el][claude-code-ide]] for Claude Code in a terminal. The conversations Claude Code and Codex record are listed too, closed ones included, whichever front end ran them; opening one resumes it in yours (see [[*Closed conversations][Closed conversations]]). One prompt buffer, =*drover*=, writes to any of them: it gathers context from where you work, lets you choose the agent, model, effort and directory, and sends to a running session or starts a new one.

* Requirements

- Emacs 31.1, a Nerd Font for the status icons, [[https://github.com/magit/transient][transient]] for the menus, [[https://github.com/magit/magit][magit-section]] for the session list, and [[https://github.com/vleonbonnet/opencode.el][opencode.el]] 2.0, whose =opencode-state= session registry gives the OpenCode sessions; Emacs 31.1 is what opencode.el needs.
- Optionally, for ACP agents (Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, Gemini CLI), agent-shell; for Claude Code in a terminal, claude-code-ide, with [[https://github.com/dakra/ghostel][ghostel]] for its statuses. Drover does not require them: their backends compile without them and load with their front end.

* Installation

With elpaca or straight, opencode.el's recipe given too, since drover requires it:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (use-package opencode
    :ensure (:host github :repo "vleonbonnet/opencode.el"))
  (use-package drover
    :ensure (:host github :repo "vleonbonnet/drover")
    :hook (elpaca-after-init . drover-attention-mode)
    :bind ("C-'" . drover-dispatch))
#+end_src

The OpenCode backend loads with opencode.el, which drover does not load before you do. Its sessions come from opencode.el's registry, which follows the shared OpenCode service whether or not a session has a buffer; drover opens the service's event stream when it starts. opencode.el's hints then name drover's keys, such as "C-' A answers it" for a pending question, through =opencode-command-keys-function=, unless you set that option yourself.

* Statuses

| Icon (Nerd Font)     | Status    | Meaning                                                 |
|----------------------+-----------+---------------------------------------------------------|
| =nf-md-help_circle=  | Needs you | A permission request or question waits for you          |
| =nf-fa-times_circle= | Failed    | A turn failed while you were not looking at the session |
| =nf-md-check_circle= | Done      | A turn ended while you were not looking at the session  |
| =nf-fa-refresh=      | Retrying  | The running turn waits to retry a failed request        |
| =nf-md-timer_sand=   | Working   | A turn runs                                             |
| =nf-fa-circle_o=     | Idle      | Nothing to do                                           |

=drover-status-glyphs= changes them; the icons need a Nerd Font. In the list, the icons of Working and Retrying are animated (see [[*Animated icons][Animated icons]]).

The first three need you (=drover-attention-statuses=). Sessions are ranked by =drover-status-order=, and among those that need you the longest wait comes first. Whether you saw a finished turn is the backend's to know: OpenCode records it in the service, so a turn read in any client, the TUI, the desktop app or Emacs, is no longer Done.

Subagent sessions show under their parent, their icon under its title. Which of them show, =drover-show-subagents= says, and =U= in the list cycles it:

| Mode     | Shows                                                                                          |
|----------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =all=    | every subagent                                                                                 |
| =active= | the subagents in the middle of a turn: working, waiting to retry, or waiting for you (default) |
| =none=   | no subagent                                                                                    |

The list says nothing of the subagents it hides: the mode is the View menu's to show, and the list's mode line adds =+subagents= or =-subagents= while not on =active=. A subagent whose turn ended, unseen or not, is not active: it hides by default, and its parent no longer stays among the Done sessions because of it. A parent goes where the most urgent session of its visible tree belongs, so one whose subagent waits for you is listed with the sessions that need you, the subagent beneath it; a hidden subagent never places its parent in a group. A subagent matching the filter shows under its parent whatever the mode, even =none=, since it explains why its tree is listed. The mode-line count, =drover-next= and =drover-select= follow the mode too, the filter aside: they count, visit and offer the sessions the list would show without a filter, and every subagent waiting for you whatever the mode, since it blocks its parent until you answer (=drover-surfaced-sessions=).

Shown or not, a session whose subagents (at any depth) are active says so beside its status icon:

| Icon (Nerd Font)       | Means                            |
|------------------------+----------------------------------|
| =nf-md-robot_confused= | subagents wait for you           |
| =nf-md-robot=          | subagents work, or wait to retry |

The icons stay still and tell no number: their tooltip names the subagents and tells how many do each. =drover-subagent-glyphs= changes the icons. This is how your OpenCode session shows that a subagent of its runs while it is itself idle.

* Attention

=drover-attention-mode= shows how many sessions need you in the mode line, as other indicators show a count: a robot (=drover-attention-glyph=, the emoji 🤖), a space and the count, such as =🤖 3=, in the face of the most urgent status among them, and nothing when no session needs you. It counts the sessions the list shows under its subagent mode, and the subagents waiting for you whatever the mode (see [[*Statuses][Statuses]]). With a Nerd Font, a Nerd icon may suit your mode line better, such as ="\U000F16A4"= (=nf-md-robot_excited_outline=): an icon drawn wider than its room, as the Nerd Fonts but their Mono variants draw icons, is kept off the count, measured once per font. The count begins with the robot: what parts it from the indicator before it is your mode line's. Its tooltip lists the sessions by status; clicking it runs =drover-next=, the right button =drover-select=.

- =drover-next= visits the most urgent session, among those counted. Pressed again at once, it moves to the next one in the order of the first press, so a session changing status meanwhile does not reshuffle the round.
- =drover-select= picks any session the list shows under its subagent mode, and any subagent waiting for you, with completion, grouped by status and annotated with its project, branch, age and what it waits for. With a prefix argument it opens the session in another window.
- =drover-mark-all-seen= marks every Done and Failed session seen after confirming how many (=drover-catch-up-statuses=); with a numeric prefix argument, only those that ended at least that many hours ago. Useful to start from a clean slate, or after a day away.

* Keys

Drover binds no global key. Its commands are gathered in one menu, =drover-dispatch=; bind it to a key you can reach from anywhere, for instance:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (keymap-global-set "C-'" #'drover-dispatch)
#+end_src

or, with =use-package=, =:bind ("C-'" . drover-dispatch)=. The keys come in three sets, and no key belongs to two, so the menu holds them all: the global keys everywhere, the session keys in lists of sessions and in prompt buffers, the prompt keys in prompt buffers. What does not apply where you are is greyed out rather than hidden, so the menu keeps its shape. With the OpenCode backend, it reads:

#+begin_example
  Global
   j Next          l List              a Agent       C Compact
   b Select        L Side list         m Model       f Fork
   " Prompt        p Project           e Effort      . Last answer
   n Task prompt   S Search sessions   d Directory   O Open link
   ' Show prompt

  Session
   o Other window   A Answer        r Rename      U Subagents
   u Parent         K Interrupt     x Delete      V View
   c Subagent       s Mark seen     z Archive
   v Peek           + New session   Z Unarchive
                    g Refresh

  Prompt
   RET Send              / Slash command   t Revert here
   S-<return> Schedule   @ Mention         T Cancel revert
   i Attach              y Copy block
   k Clear
#+end_example

** Session keys

In every list of sessions (drover's list, and the lists of other front ends that turn on =drover-session-keys-mode=) these keys act on the session at point directly; in a prompt buffer, such as an OpenCode session buffer, they act on its session through the menu.

| Key | Command                    | Action                                              |
|-----+----------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------|
| =o= | =drover-open-other-window= | open it in another window than =RET= would          |
| =A= | =drover-respond=           | answer its question or permission request, in place |
| =K= | =drover-interrupt=         | interrupt its turn, after confirmation              |
| =s= | =drover-mark-seen=         | mark its ended turn seen                            |
| =u= | =drover-open-parent=       | open its parent session                             |
| =c= | =drover-open-subagent=     | pick one of its subagent sessions and open it       |
| =r= | =drover-rename=            | rename it                                           |
| =x= | =drover-delete=            | delete it for good, after confirmation              |
| =z= | =drover-archive=           | archive it, after confirmation                      |
| =Z= | =drover-unarchive=         | restore it from the archive                         |
| =g= | =drover-refresh=           | refresh the list, or reload the session's buffer    |
| =+= | =drover-new-session=       | start a session in its directory                    |
| =v= | =drover-peek=              | preview where it stands, without opening (lists)    |
| =U= | =drover-toggle-subagents=  | show all, active or no subagents, in turn (lists)   |
| =V= | =drover-view=              | the View menu (drover's list)                       |

=RET= opens the session at point, and the usual keys of each list stay: =n= and =p= move, =TAB= folds, =q= quits. =RET= opens it where the list sends sessions: from a side window, in the window where you work; from an ordinary window, in the list's own window. =o= opens it in another window than that one: the window already showing the session, else the one used last, else a window split from it; never in a side window, a window dedicated to its buffer or either list. Drover chooses the window and selects it before the backend shows the session, so sessions of every backend open alike. In lists drover does not draw, =drover-session-keys-mode= adds these keys. opencode.el lists no sessions of its own: drover's list shows them.

=z= archives a session in its front end's archive, such as OpenCode's, or in drover's own when its front end has none (see [[*Archived sessions][Archived sessions]]); =Z= restores it, from the list showing archived sessions, or from the session's buffer.

=s=, =K=, =z=, =Z= and =x= act on every session selected, when a region selects several (see [[*Selecting sessions][Selecting sessions]]): =K= on those that run a turn, =s= on those whose turn ended unseen, =z= on those not archived, =Z= on those archived, with one confirmation naming them all, such as =Archive 3 sessions: A, B, C?=, and one report, such as =Archived 3 sessions=, naming those that failed without stopping the others.

To store an Org link to the session at point, use Org's own =org-store-link= (=C-c l=) with a link type of your own whose store function reads =drover-session-at-point=. Drover makes its links the same way (=drover-session-link=): =M-w= in the list copies them, and the prompt quotes them.

** The menu beside the list

The session keys of the menu act on the session the current buffer tells: the one at point in a list, the buffer's own in a session buffer. In a buffer telling no session, such as the prompt, =*drover*=, or a file you edit, while one of drover's lists, the sidebar or the other, shows in a window of the frame (the one used last, if both do), they act on that list instead: on the session at point of the list's window, as if typed there, and so do the list's own entries, =v=, =U= and =V=. A session buffer keeps acting on its own session. The global and prompt keys keep to the current buffer.

The List group, whether the menu opened in the list or beside it, moves the point of the list's window, the menu staying open and following the new session at point: its title names it, and what applies to it is greyed out anew. Follow mode shows each session as you go, its title taking the colour of the session you work in, as moving in the list would.

*** The List group

While the menu acts on drover's list, here or beside, it shows a fourth group, named after the session at point; there =RET= opens that session rather than send, so the Prompt group leaves it out:

#+begin_example
  Prompt
   S-<return> Schedule   / Slash command   t Revert here
   i Attach              @ Mention         T Cancel revert
   k Clear               y Copy block

  List: Fix the login redirect
   C-n Next session       M-n Next group       M-< First session   RET Open
   C-p Previous session   M-p Previous group   M-> Last session
#+end_example

| Key   | Command                        | Action                                          |
|-------+--------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------|
| =C-n= | =drover-list-next-session=     | the next session, over group headings           |
| =C-p= | =drover-list-previous-session= | the previous session                            |
| =M-n= | =drover-list-next-group=       | the first session of the next group             |
| =M-p= | =drover-list-previous-group=   | the first session of the previous group         |
| =M-<= | =drover-list-first-session=    | the first session                               |
| =M->= | =drover-list-last-session=     | the last session in sight                       |
| =RET= | =drover-open-at-point=         | open the session at point, as =RET= in the list |

Sessions of collapsed groups are passed over. =RET= opens the session as =RET= in the list would: from a side window, in the window where you work; from an ordinary window, in that window, or with =C-u=, the menu's prefix argument, in another one. In a buffer sending prompts, such as =*drover*=, =RET= stays Send: a prompt lost to a slip of the finger costs more than reaching for =o=, which opens the list's session in another window. These keys bind nothing in the list itself, where Magit's =n=, =p=, =M-n= and =M-p= move.

The prompt keys, and =O=, apply only in the prompt buffer itself, since they work on what you see there.

** Adding to the menu

Add your own commands with =transient-append-suffix= and =transient-insert-suffix=, placed relative to a key of the menu. These places stay put:

- after ='=, the last of drover's ways to pick a session or prompt, more of them, chained one after the other: =#= after ='=, and so on (after =b= too, before the prompt's);
- after =p=, more lists of sessions: the OpenCode backend appends =S= there;
- after =O=, more prompt commands.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (transient-append-suffix 'drover-dispatch "'"
    '("#" "Scheduled prompts" my-scheduled-prompts))
#+end_src

=drover-dispatch-session=, =drover-dispatch-list-p=, =drover-dispatch-view-p= and =drover-dispatch-prompt-p= are predicates for the =:inapt-if-not= of such entries.

* The list

=drover-list= shows every session in a Magit-style buffer, =*drover sessions*=, in an ordinary window; =drover-list-side= shows the sidebar, a list of its own, =*drover sidebar*=, in a side window beside your work (=drover-list-side-window-side=, =drover-list-side-window-width=), or hides it, and with a prefix argument moves between it and your work. Both may show at once, each laid out for its own width, typically two lines per session in the sidebar and columns in the other; each has its own point, its own sessions expanded and groups collapsed, and its own follow mode, and the keys act in the list you type them in. What they list, how they sort, group and filter, and which subagents and archived sessions show are the same for both: the View menu changes them for both. Groups and sessions are sections: =TAB= folds a group, or recaps the last turn of a session (see [[*The last turn][The last turn]]). The Idle group starts collapsed (=drover-list-collapsed-groups=); what you collapse or expand survives refreshes.

The buffer shows sessions, and how many each group holds, nothing else: no line counts them at the top, as the mode line counts the sessions that need you (see [[*Attention][Attention]]); which subagents show, the filter, the sort and the keys are the View menu's to show (=V=), and the mode line's to hint at, such as =by status filtered=. A filter leaving every session out says =No session matches the filter.=, and a list without sessions =No sessions.=; the View menu tells which filter, and clears it.

From the sidebar, =RET= opens the session at point in the window where you work, never in the other list, =o= in another window than that one, split from it when there is none, =C-o= shows it where =RET= would and leaves you in the sidebar, and follow mode shows each session there as point reaches it. In either list, the colour of a title tells where its session is (see [[*Where sessions are][Where sessions are]]).

** What a line shows

The list is drawn in a proportional font (the face =drover-list=), laid out in pixels, and what a line shows depends on the width of its window. =drover-list-fields= ranks the fields a line may show and the room each needs:

| Field    | Column   | Shows                                                                       | Rank | In two lines          |
|----------+----------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+------+-----------------------|
| title    | Session  | the status icon, the icon of active subagents and the title, always         |    0 | first line            |
| age      | Age      | how long it has been active, or needed you                                  |    1 | first line, right     |
| cache    | Cache    | the prompt cache's lifetime left: a short bar and the time left             |    2 | the line's background |
| summary  | Summary  | what it waits for                                                           |    3 | second line           |
| model    | Model    | the model, compact, such as =Opus 5.5=, then its effort, dim when a default |    4 |                       |
| context  | Context  | the context's size, such as =182k=, after a short bar of how full it is     |    5 |                       |
| provider | Provider | the provider serving the model, as an icon                                  |    6 |                       |
| location | Project  | the project                                                                 |    7 |                       |
| branch   | Branch   | the Git branch, unless the repository's default one                         |    8 |                       |
| agent    | Agent    | the agent, as =@build=, in its colour                                       |    9 |                       |
| created  | Created  | how long ago it was created                                                 |   10 |                       |

The list shows the fields with something to show, by rank, as long as they fit, as aligned columns, but those you hide (=drover-list-hidden-fields=, or the View menu's Columns). A window of about 75 columns shows the title, age, cache, summary, model and effort, context and provider; one of about 100, every field, the branch's a few columns more. When not even the summary fits beside the title, age and cache, each session takes two lines instead: its icons, title and age, and under them, for a session that needs you, what it waits for; the model, provider and context then show in its details, =TAB=, and its tooltip. The layout is computed again whenever the window's width changes. One space parts each icon from the next and from the title, and columns stay aligned to the pixel whatever the icons before them.

In columns, the header line names each column (the =:label= of its field), aligned to it to the pixel, past line numbers if you show them; hover a name to read what its column shows. A column is at least as wide as its name, room allowing. Two lines have no header line.

A column is as wide as what it shows, up to its field's =:max=. On a line too narrow for every column, the summary gives up room first, then the names of the columns of short texts, then the title, then the project, branch, agent and model, in proportion; the ages, context, cache and provider are never cut, nor the model's effort, which keeps its place while the model's name is cut, such as =Opus… high=. A column gives up only the room missing: no column is narrowed while the line has room.

The branch shows only for the sessions working on another branch than their repository's default one: the one the repository's remote names (=refs/remotes/origin/HEAD=, read from the file system, as is every branch, without running Git), else =main= or =master= (=drover-default-branches=). When every session works on its default branch, the column takes no room; a session's details tell the branch its line leaves out.

The agent is drawn as =@build=, bold, the =@= in its face too, so that it reads as one word, as an agent mentioned in a prompt is spelt. OpenCode sessions show their agent as opencode.el's mode line does (=opencode-agent-display=): the catalog's name, in the agent's configured colour, else in a face of =opencode-mode-line-agent-faces= by its place among the primary agents; plain until the catalog arrives. Other backends' agents take a face of =drover-agent-faces= by their name, by default those same faces when opencode.el is loaded.

The model's effort is green when chosen (=drover-prompt-picked=) and dim when it is the model's default (=drover-prompt-inherited=), as in the prompt's header; its tooltip says which. An agent-shell shell left on its agent's own =default= level, as Claude Code's agent offers, shows the level its model takes by default, dim, as Claude Code's catalog or what drover learnt of the model tells it, and no effort when neither does. The provider's icon comes from the backend, such as opencode.el's =opencode-provider-icons=, its tooltip naming it. The context's bar fills as the context nears the model's size; near a long-context price tier, past which requests cost more, its size turns to =drover-context-near=, past it to =drover-context-over=, and the bar's filled part takes that colour; the tooltip tells the tokens, the model's limit and the tier. A size the service only estimated, as OpenCode does after compacting a conversation until its next step measures it, begins with =~=, such as =~41k=, and its tooltip says so. The context costs a request per session to some backends, OpenCode's among them: the list asks for it only for the sessions in sight, those of expanded groups, while columns may show it.

Each icon takes the room its font draws it in, which may be wider than the room the font gives it: the Nerd Fonts but their Mono variants draw icons up to half a cell past their advance, and drover measures that ink in the fonts of the list's window, so no text is drawn on an icon. Every status icon takes the room of the widest, so titles line up whatever the status. A subagent's icon stands under its parent's title, past the icons of the parent's active subagents.

** Where sessions are

The colour of a session's title tells where the session is in Emacs:

| Face                    | Inherits                       | The session                                                                                            |
|-------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| none                    |                                | has no buffer                                                                                          |
| =drover-open-session=   | =font-lock-keyword-face=       | has a buffer no window of a visible frame shows                                                        |
| =drover-shown-session=  | =font-lock-type-face=          | shows in a window of a visible frame                                                                   |
| =drover-active-session= | =font-lock-function-name-face= | shows in the window you work in: the selected one, or the one selected before a list or the minibuffer |

The three faces inherit sibling faces of font lock, which themes tell apart from one another and from the default foreground; none of them is a status's, so a title never reads as a status: the faces of =success=, =warning= and =error= colour the Done, Needs you and Failed icons. =font-lock-function-name-face= names where you are elsewhere too: =which-function-mode= shows the function point is in with it. Over an idle session's dimming, they lend the dimming what they do not set.

As you select windows and show buffers in them, only the titles whose session changed place take their new face, in place; the list is not drawn anew, but when a face would change the width of a title, as a bold one would. A buffer opening or closing is a change its backend reports, which redraws the list. =drover-list-current=, the face of the former highlight of the session beside the list, is an obsolete alias of =drover-active-session=.

** The last turn

Expanded, a session recaps its last turn under its title, in the list's font:

#+begin_example
  ✓  Fix the login redirect          plan  Opus 5.5 high   1h
     Prompt   The login page sends users back to /home after they sign
              in; keep the page they came from.
     Reply    The redirect now keeps the page users came from, in a
              signed parameter, …
     Changes  +42 −7 in 3 files: auth/login.go, auth/redirect.go, …
#+end_example

- what the session waits for, every question or permission request in full, and the error that ended its turn or that it retries after;
- the prompt it was given last, and the agent's newest reply since, a few lines each, wrapped to the window (=drover-list-recap-lines=): =v= previews the whole reply, =RET= opens the session;
- the files the turn changed, with the lines added and removed when the backend knows them;
- what the session's line leaves out at this width, such as the model, provider and context in a side window, and the directory of a session without a project.

The recap is fetched as the session expands, without waiting: =loading…= shows meanwhile. It is kept until the session changes, fetched again then at most every two seconds while the session stays expanded, and anew by =g=. OpenCode sessions ask the service (=opencode-session-last-turn=), Claude Code conversations, in a terminal or closed, read their transcript, closed Codex ones their rollout, and agent-shell's shells tell what their events said since drover followed them.

** Prompt caches

A session whose provider still holds its prompt cache, so that its next turn costs less if sent before the cache expires, shows how much of the cache's lifetime is left: in columns, as a short bar and the time left, such as =4m=, text you may copy; in two lines, as a line under its line, drawn from the left and shrinking as the cache runs out. Both take the colour of =drover-cache-bar=, which inherits =success=: the short bar fills with it, and the line is underlined in it, so that both show over the highlight of the current line and the region, which set only a background. Its tooltip tells how the cache stands as you read it, such as =Prompt cache: expires in 4m (1h window), 120k of 130k tokens cached=: the window is how long the provider keeps it after each turn, and the tokens what share of the conversation the next turn reads from it. An expired or unknown cache shows nothing. While the list is shown and a cache is live, a timer redraws the caches alone, every =drover-list-cache-interval= seconds.

** Animated icons

The icons of Working and Retrying sessions are animated in the list: each shows the frames of =drover-animations= in turn, by default the eight phases of the moon and a clock whose hand turns (Nerd Font icons, any string will do). Each frame differs little from the one before, so they move rather than blink: a lone solid frame among outlined ones, such as a full hourglass, would read as a blink. Every frame takes the room of the widest, so nothing after it moves in the proportional font. As for the bars of prompt caches, a timer changes only how those few characters display, every =drover-list-animation-interval= seconds, 0.15 by default (a moon cycle in 1.2 seconds), and only while a shown list animates something; =nil= keeps the icons still.

** The View menu

=V= opens the View menu, which stays open as you change its settings and redraws the list at once:

#+begin_example
  Sort by                    Group by   Filter by        Toggles
   o urgency                  g status   p project any    F follow [off|on]
   r [ascending|descending]              s status any     U subagents [all|active|none]
                                         a agent any      O older [hidden|shown]
                                         m model any      A archived [hidden|shown]
                                         v provider any
                                         d updated any
                                         f text any
                                         c clear

  Columns
   -l location  -b branch  -a agent  -m model  -r provider  -x context  -c created  -t age  -p cache  -s summary
#+end_example

- Sort by urgency, the most urgent first, by title, project, branch, agent, model, provider, last activity or creation, either way. Subagents stay under their parent: the trees are sorted, then the subagents of each session among themselves.
- Group by status, project, branch, agent, model, provider (each group named after the provider, behind its icon), last activity (today, yesterday, this week, older), or nothing.
- Filter by project, status, agent, model, provider (by name, among the providers of the sessions drover knows), last activity (today, the last 24 hours, 7 or 30 days, or any age such as =3d=) and a text found in the title, summary, project or branch. An empty answer removes a filter; =c= clears them all. A subagent that matches shows under its parent, even while subagents are hidden.
- Toggle follow mode, which subagents show (all, the active ones, none: =U= cycles them, as in the list), older sessions: those a backend keeps beyond the ones it follows, such as every unarchived OpenCode session, and archived sessions (see [[*Archived sessions][Archived sessions]]). Both are fetched when shown, and again by =g=.
- Choose the columns: each switch lets a field show, highlighted, or hides it, dimmed, at once; the list then chooses by width among the fields shown. The title always shows. The switches change =drover-list-hidden-fields= for the session; customize it to keep your choice.

The lists follow sessions as they change while they are displayed; hidden, a list notes which sessions changed, draws nothing meanwhile, and draws them in place once shown again, or whole when many did. Only what changed is drawn: the lines of the sessions that changed, in place, and a session moving, coming or going alone, so that a list of thousands of sessions follows a running turn in a millisecond or two; with the sidebar and the other list both shown, each draws its own lines, but what a change makes is made once for both. A list is drawn whole only when its columns change width, a setting changes, or =g= refreshes it; =g= draws from what the backends reported, then lists every session anew, for what they change without telling, such as a branch, and draws what changed. The sessions of a collapsed group, such as Idle, often most of them, are drawn once it is expanded, searched with Isearch, copied or selected, and otherwise a few at a time while Emacs is idle and the list shown, stopping as you type (=drover-list-deferred-delay=), so that Occur or Consult find them too.

** Archived sessions

Archived sessions leave the list, the mode-line count and every session picker. The View menu's =A= shows them in the list too, dimmed, after =drover-archived-glyph= (=nf-md-archive=), whose tooltip tells how long ago they were archived; grouped by status, they gather in a group of their own, Archived, last, whose heading counts them, and they never count as needing you. They are fetched when shown, without waiting, and again by =g=; =Z= restores those selected, or the one at point.

Each front end archives what it can:

| Front end           | =z= archives                                            | =Z= restores                                    | =x= deletes                                                        | =f= forks                                                 | =C= compacts                                               |
|---------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------|
| opencode.el         | in OpenCode, for every client                           | in OpenCode                                     | in OpenCode                                                        | in OpenCode                                               | in OpenCode                                                |
| agent-shell         | in drover's own archive                                 | from drover's archive                           | through the agent, ACP's =session/delete=, then closes the shell   | through the agent, ACP's =session/fork=, when it can fork | sends =/compact=, when the agent offers that command       |
| claude-code-ide     | in drover's own archive                                 | from drover's archive                           | closes the terminal, then deletes the transcript and its directory | =claude --resume ID --fork-session=, in a new terminal    | types =/compact= once the turn ended and the input is read |
| Claude Code, closed | in drover's own archive                                 | from drover's archive                           | deletes the transcript and its directory, unless it runs elsewhere | as claude-code-ide does, when it is loaded                | once resumed                                               |
| Codex, closed       | in Codex, =codex archive=; without the CLI, in drover's | in Codex, =codex unarchive=; else from drover's | in Codex, =codex delete=, with the CLI                             |                                                           | once resumed                                               |

Drover's own archive holds, by conversation, the sessions of front ends without an archive of their own, in =archive.eld= of =drover-state-directory=, with what the list shows of them: an archived terminal still shows, among the archived sessions, once its buffer is gone, as the conversation Claude Code recorded, which opens, is deleted or forked from there. Archiving a running shell or terminal does not stop it. =Z= restores a conversation whose terminal or shell is gone as a closed one (see [[*Closed conversations][Closed conversations]]); one that nothing lists any more, as a Gemini CLI shell's, would leave every list: =Z= refuses, saying so, and it stays among the archived sessions. A conversation two front ends show leaves both. A session whose conversation is not known yet, such as a terminal Claude Code has not recorded, cannot be archived. Deleting a Claude Code session in a terminal deletes what Claude Code's own SDK deletes, the transcript under =~/.claude/projects/= and the directory of its subagents beside it, moved to the trash when =delete-by-moving-to-trash= says so, and only once drover found exactly one transcript of that identifier.

** Selecting sessions

An active region selects the sessions whose lines it spans, those the list highlights, as Magit selects sections: set the mark on a session's line and move a line down, and both sessions are selected, wherever on their lines the region begins and ends. A group's heading selected stands for its sessions, so =C-SPC= on the heading of Done selects every Done session. A region running from one group into another selects nothing, and shows as a region does in other buffers; the commands then act on the session at point. The region and what it selects survive the redraws a working session causes.

- =s=, =K=, =z=, =Z= and =x= act on every session selected (see [[*Session keys][Session keys]]), from the list or from =C-'=, whose List group then tells how many are selected; acting ends the selection.
- =M-w= copies the Org links of the sessions selected, one per line, else that of the session at point; a session no link type stores is copied by its title, and a region selecting no session is copied as text.
- =C-' "= quotes them in the prompt (see [[*The prompt][The prompt]]).

* Closed conversations

Claude Code and Codex record every conversation, whichever front end ran it, in Emacs or not: Claude Code a transcript under =~/.claude/projects/=, Codex a rollout under =~/.codex/sessions/=. Drover lists them, with claude-code-ide or agent-shell loaded, as the CLIs' own pickers list them: titled as the CLI titled them, else by their first prompt, in their directory and branch, with the model and effort of their last turn, idle, in the Idle group; Codex's archived ones among the archived sessions, and Claude Code's subagents under their conversation. A conversation a terminal or shell follows is that front end's session, listed once, with its live status; closed, it is listed again as recorded, in place.

- =RET= resumes it: in claude-code-ide, =claude --resume ID= in a terminal started in its directory; in agent-shell, a shell of its engine loading it by its identifier. Of the front ends loaded, the one it ran in comes first, as Claude Code's transcript tells it, unless =drover-resume-front-ends= names the ones you prefer, such as =((claude-code agent-shell))=. One a front end follows opens there.
- Its recap and =v= preview come from the end of its transcript or rollout, without resuming it.
- A session =claude agents --json= lists as running, in the background or in another terminal, shows working, or waiting with =claude attach ID= as what it waits for; it is attached to there, not resumed here. Drover runs the command at most every =drover-claude-code-agents-interval= seconds, 30, while a list shows; nil never runs it.
- =z=, =Z= and =x= archive, restore and delete them as the table of [[*Archived sessions][Archived sessions]] says; Codex's through its CLI, =drover-codex-program=, when found.

Drover reads only what it needs of each file, its head and tail, then what was appended, and looks at the files every =drover-transcript-scan-interval= seconds, in slices that give way to your typing: those of the last day at every look, every file every eighth look. What it read it keeps in =drover-state-directory=, =claude-code-transcripts.eld= and =codex-rollouts.eld=, a cache checked against the files, so that an Emacs starting reads only what changed. =drover-claude-code-history-days= and =drover-codex-history-days= move conversations idle longer among the older sessions, shown on demand (the View menu's =O=); nil, the default, lists them all. =drover-codex-include-non-interactive= lists what =codex exec= ran too.

* The prompt

=C-' "= (=drover-prompt-dwim=) opens =*drover*=, an Org buffer, with a link to the line at point and the region quoted in a code block, an Org heading's line as any other; called again from elsewhere, it adds that context where you left point in the prompt. =C-' n= (=drover-prompt-org-task=) starts a planning prompt for the Org task point is in, from anywhere in its entry, folded or narrowed (=drover-prompt-task-format=, =drover-prompt-task-agent=), asking before it replaces a draft. =C-' '= (=drover-prompt-toggle=) shows or hides the prompt.

In the list, =C-' "= quotes the sessions selected, or the one at point, for an agent to read: an item per session, its Org link, titled after it, how it stands, and its last reply in a Markdown block, cut to =drover-prompt-quote-reply-lines= lines and =drover-prompt-quote-reply-characters= characters with a note telling how much is left out. The replies are fetched from the backends all at once, the echo area counting them as they arrive; a backend not answering within =drover-prompt-quote-timeout= seconds leaves its session quoted without one:

#+begin_example
  Agent sessions:
  - [[agent-session:opencode/ses_1][Fix the login redirect]] — done
    Last reply:
    ,#+begin_src markdown
    The redirect now keeps the page users came from.
    ,#+end_src
  - [[agent-session:opencode/ses_2][Bump the SDK]] — waiting for an answer: asks: Which version?
#+end_example

The header line shows what the prompt would run: directory, agent, model and effort, dim when inherited from the engine's defaults, bold when chosen with =C-' d=, =a=, =m= and =e=. From another buffer these keys choose for the next prompt. =a= switches directly when only two agents are offered.

=C-' RET= sends: it asks for the target among the running sessions, then the new sessions of =drover-prompt-new-targets=, each labelled with what it would run; in that picker =C-'= changes the options. Explicit choices are applied to the target before the prompt goes out. With an active region, only the region is sent and the rest stays for another send. A send that sends nothing, cancelled by a check, leaves the prompt as it was. =C-' k= clears the text and keeps the choices; =C-' i= attaches a buffer, quoted, or a project file, linked.

* Engines

An engine is the agent program a session runs: =opencode=, =claude-code=, =codex= or =gemini-cli=. Engines, not front ends, know what a new session runs: their models, their startup defaults (read from Claude Code's settings layers, Codex's =config.toml=, OpenCode's catalogs), their agents, and when they last took a prompt, which picks the engine the header shows. Each front end registers the engines it runs, so agent-shell can run any of them while claude-code-ide runs Claude Code only.

The models the prompt offers come from live sessions: the models each agent-shell shell offers, the catalog the Claude Code CLI caches while claude-code-ide runs a session, and OpenCode's own catalog. Drover asks no vendor API and reads no credential. Each model offers its own efforts, never another's: a shell tells the thought levels of the model it runs only, so the others take theirs from what their engine keeps locally, the effort levels of each model in Claude Code's catalog, the reasoning levels Codex's agent lists for each model and its models cache, and are otherwise unknown, which the picker's annotation says (={effort unknown}=) and the effort key tells rather than offering levels the model may not take. A Claude model is one model whatever names it: agent-shell's Claude agent lists aliases, such as =opus= or =default=, which the catalog resolves to the model they stand for, as the CLI does, so the picker shows =Opus 5.5= once, and agent-shell is sent the alias it lists, claude-code-ide the model's identifier. What live sessions offered is remembered on disk, in =models.eld= of =drover-state-directory= (=drover/= in =user-emacs-directory=, or no-littering's =var/drover/=), so the picker offers an engine's models when none of its sessions runs, each annotated with the day a session last offered it ("remembered from 2026-10-29"). A live answer always wins and replaces the models remembered, but for what it does not tell of a model, such as the efforts of a model the shell no longer runs, or the alias a front end takes for it: those the memory keeps, so it learns each model's efforts as you switch models. A picker open while a shell starts shows its models as they come. Set =drover-remember-models= to nil to keep nothing beyond the Emacs session, and =M-x drover-forget-models= forgets what was kept. =drover-claude-code-oauth-catalog= is obsolete and ignored.

Two front ends may show one conversation, such as an OpenCode session open in opencode.el and in agent-shell: drover lists it once, from the front end whose buffer you used last.

* Backends

A backend registers with =drover-register-backend=, giving functions that list its sessions as =drover-session= records and act on them (open, answer, interrupt, archive, unarchive, mark seen, preview, start, rename, delete, fork, compact), tell which of them apply to a session (=:supports=), fetch its older sessions (=:history=), its archived ones (=:archived=) and the recap of a session's last turn (=:recap=, see =drover-recap=); a backend listing conversations as their engine recorded them says so (=:recorded=), and yields to a front end following one. It reports changes with =drover-backend-changed=, naming the session when it can: with =:session=, a function returning the record of one session, drover then asks for that record alone rather than for every session, and lists draw that session alone. Drover archives the sessions of a backend without =:archive= itself. A record names its engine and the engine's identifier of the conversation (=engine=, =engine-id=), whether and when it was archived (=archived=), and may tell when the session's prompt cache expires, how long it lasts and how much of the prompt it holds (=cache-expires=, =cache-ttl=, =cache-tokens=, =cache-prompt-tokens=), and what the session runs: its agent as lists show it, in its faces (=agent-display=), its model, compact, its effort and whether that is the model's default (=model=, =effort=, =effort-default=), its provider (=provider=, a compact display such as an icon, and =provider-name=), and its context (=context-tokens=, =context-limit=, =context-tier=, and =context-estimated= when the size is the service's estimate after a compaction). What costs a request to know, such as the context, a backend fetches when asked by =:prefetch=, for the sessions a view shows. For the prompt it may offer =:engines=, =:create=, =:check=, =:apply-options=, =:send=, =:options= and =:settable=; an engine registers with =drover-register-engine= and its models with =drover-register-model-source=, and a front end how it resumes an engine's conversations with =drover-register-resumer=. See =doc/design.org=.

A front end also fits its buffers into the keys. A list of sessions sets =drover-session-at-point-function= and turns on =drover-session-keys-mode=; a prompt buffer sets =drover-session-at-point-function= to its own session and declares the prompt actions it implements with =drover-prompt-define=, such as =(drover-prompt-define 'send #'my-send)=.
